News & Media

Announcements, match recaps, committee updates, and press mentions from around the club.

Announcement

2026 team tryouts are now open

Applications close on 18 July. All game programs are recruiting, from first-timers to competitive-ready players.

Sign-ups are open to all UTAR students regardless of prior competitive experience. Tryout sessions will run across two weekends, with results and squad placements announced the following week on this page and in the club Discord.

Match recap

PUBG squad secures runner-up at inter-varsity cup

A tight final circle came down to the last two squads standing, with Utar Gaming finishing just short of the title.

The team placed first in three of five matches across the group stage before facing a stacked finals bracket. Coaches praised the squad's rotation discipline, and the roster will carry the momentum into the upcoming Nexus Open.

Feature

Meet the committee: new vice president for 2026

We sat down with the club's newly appointed Vice President to talk goals for the year ahead.

Priorities for the term include expanding the coaching pipeline, growing casual community events, and formalising a sponsorship track for future tournaments. A full write-up will follow in the next committee update.

Press

Utar Gaming featured in campus tech bulletin

The university's tech bulletin covered the club's growth and its role in campus student life this semester.

The feature highlighted the club's member growth over the past year and its collaboration with other student societies on joint LAN events, positioning Utar Gaming as one of the more active technology-adjacent clubs on campus.

Announcement

Nexus Open 2026 registration deadline extended

Due to demand, team registration for the Nexus Open has been extended by one week.

Teams that have already registered do not need to take any action. New teams can register through the Tournaments page before the new closing date; slots are limited per game category.

Match recap

Mobile Legends team wins community showdown

A clean sweep in the grand finals capped off a strong showing at this month's community showdown night.

The roster went undefeated across the bracket, with the jungle-support duo drawing praise for objective control. Highlights from the run are available on the Gallery & Highlights page.

Welcome to UTAR Gaming

More than a team. A community built around gaming.

UTAR Gaming E-Sports Club brings together students who share an interest in gaming and esports.

The club provides opportunities to participate in gaming activities, tournaments, and community events while developing teamwork, communication, leadership, and other valuable skills through esports.

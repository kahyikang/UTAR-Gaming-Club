The people behind the plays

Built by students, powered by passion.

Utar Gaming is a student-driven esports community that brings together competitive players, casual gamers, content creators, casters, and event organisers. We provide a platform for students to develop their skills, connect with others, and experience esports beyond the game itself.

Our story

A serious place to grow, whatever your role.

Utar Gaming was established to create a dedicated space for UTAR students who share a passion for competitive gaming and esports. The club brings together students with different interests, from competitive players and coaches to shoutcasters, content creators, designers, and event organisers.

Through training sessions, gaming activities, tournaments, and community events, members can improve their skills while gaining practical experience in teamwork, communication, leadership, and event management.

More importantly, Utar Gaming aims to create an inclusive environment where students can meet new people, enjoy gaming together, and represent UTAR in esports activities both on and off campus.

“Play with purpose. Compete together.”

Utar Gaming club motto

What we stand for

Our mission and values.

Utar Gaming is guided by a commitment to competitive growth, inclusiveness, teamwork, and personal development.

Compete with discipline

We encourage members to train consistently, learn from feedback, respect their opponents, and approach every competition with discipline and sportsmanship.

Keep the door open

Esports is for everyone. Whether students are experienced competitors, casual players, or completely new to esports, everyone is welcome to participate and become part of the community.

Build beyond the game

Esports provides opportunities to develop valuable skills in leadership, communication, event management, broadcasting, content creation, design, and teamwork.
